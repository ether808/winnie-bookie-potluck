The house rules

How this works

  1. The weekly ingredient must feature meaningfully in your dish.
  2. Anything goes: sweet, savoury, drink, snack, main or dessert.
  3. You each make your own dish.
  4. No copying the other person's idea before the reveal.
  5. You have one week.
  6. Photographic evidence is strongly encouraged.
  7. Complaining about the ingredient is permitted. Changing it is not.
