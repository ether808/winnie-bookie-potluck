The house rules

How this works

  1. One ingredient is randomly selected every week.
  2. The spinner alternates between Winnie and Bookie.
  3. Whoever spins does NOT get to sit out. Both of you cook.
  4. The ingredient must meaningfully feature in your dish.
  5. Sweet, savoury, snack, drink, breakfast or dinner — interpret it however you want.
  6. Submit your dish privately before reveal day.
  7. On reveal day, both dishes are unveiled.
  8. You rate each other's dishes.
